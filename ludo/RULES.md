# Ludo

Race your four tokens from your yard, once round the board and up your home column to the middle. Two to six players, on a 4-player board or a 6-player board; free-for-all or in teams. Bots can fill the empty seats, or the seats can stay empty.

## Setting up
- The setup shows only the first time: for a party's first game with friends, and when the players change (CHANGE PLAYERS to a different table). **PLAY AGAIN** goes straight on with the same board, mode and partners, and so does a game started alone from its card at the table you last played (a first one: the 4-player board, free-for-all). If the players no longer fit the teams, the setup shows again.
- The host picks the **board**: the 4-player board (a cross, 52 squares round) or the 6-player board (six arms round a hexagon, 66 squares round). Five or six players need the 6-player board.
- And the **mode**: **free-for-all**, or **teams** — on the 4-player board 2 v 2, partners opposite each other; on the 6-player board 3 teams of 2 (partners opposite) or 2 teams of 3 (every other arm). Teams need every seat of the board filled. **MIX TEAMS** deals new partners.
- With fewer players than arms, the empty arms stay empty: two players on the 4-player board sit opposite each other.
- Who rolls first changes every game, round the table.

## How to play
- **Tap the die** to roll. Players take turns clockwise.
- A token leaves its yard only on a **6**, onto its start square (the coloured square with a star).
- A **6 rolls again**. Three 6s in a row, though, **lose the turn**: the third 6 moves nothing.
- Tokens move clockwise round the board, then turn up their own home column (the arrow at the tip of your arm) to the middle.
- If only one move is possible (or all of them come to the same thing, like bringing out any token on a 6), it is made for you. Otherwise every token that can move gets a ring: **tap a ring** to move that token. Where it would land is marked.
- Land on another player's token and it goes **back to its yard**.
- **Star squares are safe**, and so is every start square: nobody is sent home from them, and any number of tokens may share them.
- Your own tokens may share a square: a stack, with its count beside it (as notches on its rim when other players share the square too). There are no blocks: anyone may pass anyone.

## Getting home
- You need the **exact roll** to reach the middle: on the square before it you need a 1. A roll that would go past it cannot be played.

## Winning
- **Free-for-all:** the first to bring all four tokens home is first. Play goes on for places: second as well with three players, and third with four or more. Everyone else is placed by how far round their tokens got.
- Once every person is placed (home, or left) and only bots are still racing, the game ends there: the bots are placed by how far round they got.
- **Teams:** a team wins when **all its partners'** tokens are home. Once your own four are home you still roll on your turn, and **move a partner's tokens** instead. Partners never send each other home.
- If a player leaves: in free-for-all their tokens come off and they are placed last; in a team their tokens stay, and their partners move them on their own turns.

## Example
You roll a 6 with every token in the yard: one comes out onto your start, and you roll again — a 4. That token is the only one out, so it moves 4 by itself. Next turn you roll a 3 and land on a rival's token: back to its yard it goes.

## Controls
- Tap the die (or press **R** or Space) when it glows: it is your roll.
- Tap a ring (or press **1**–**4**) to choose a token.
- Each player's tokens have a colour and their own mark (ring, cross, triangle, diamond, square, star), the same as beside their name.
