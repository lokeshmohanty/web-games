# Snakes & Ladders

Race your token from the bottom of the board to square 100. Ladders carry you up; snakes take you down. Two to six players; bots can fill the empty seats, or the seats can stay empty.

## How to play
- Everyone starts off the board. Players take turns rolling one die.
- **Tap the die** to roll. Your token moves that many squares by itself: there is never anything to choose.
- The squares wind upward: 1 to 10 left to right along the bottom, 11 to 20 right to left above, and so on up to 100 at the top left.
- Land on the **foot of a ladder** and you climb to its top.
- Land on a **snake's head** and you slide down to its tail.
- Tokens can share a square. Nobody is knocked off.
- A 6 does not give another roll.
- Who rolls first changes every game, round the table.

## A new board every game
- The snakes and ladders are dealt fresh each game, and each board is checked before it is used: no ladder ends on a snake's head, no snake drops onto a ladder, nothing on 1 or 100, never three snake heads within six squares, ladders and snakes spread up the whole board, and each one crosses at least one row.
- Snakes and ladders come in about equal numbers, and every board is played through many times before you see it: one token takes about 35 to 45 rolls to get home, on every board.

## Winning
- You must land on **100 exactly**. A roll that would take you past it leaves your token where it is: on 97 you need a 3.
- The first to reach 100 is first. **Play goes on for places:** the others keep rolling until second place is taken too, and third as well with four or more players. A token on 100 rolls no more. (With two players the first home decides it.)
- Everyone still on the board is then placed by how far up they got.
- If a player leaves, their token comes off and they are placed last; the others play on. A place already won on 100 is kept.

## Example
You are on 94 and roll a 4: you land on 98, a snake's head, and slide down to 78. Next turn you roll a 6 and land on 84, the foot of a ladder that takes you up to 96. Now you need exactly a 4.

## Controls
- Tap the die (or press **R**) when it glows: it is your roll.
- Each token has its colour and its own mark (ring, cross, triangle, diamond, square, star), the same as beside its name.
