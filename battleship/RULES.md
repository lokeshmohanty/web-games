# Battleship

Sink every ship in your opponent's fleet before they sink yours. It is always two players, and a bot can take the other seat.

## How to play
- Each player has an 8 by 8 sea and four ships: **Carrier** (4 squares), **Cruiser** (3), **Sub** (3) and **Patrol** (2).
- Your fleet starts placed at random. Move ships if you like, then tap **COMMIT FLEET**. Ships lie across or down, never off the edge or overlapping.
- You have two and a half minutes. If time is nearly up, your fleet is committed as it stands.
- There are no turns: in each volley **both players fire at once**. You have a minute to pick a square, or you skip that volley.
- Each shot is answered *hit* or *miss* by the phone holding that fleet.
- Your fleet never leaves your phone. At the end both fleets are revealed and every answer is checked, so anyone who lied is named as a cheat.

## Scoring
- There are no points. The status line counts your hits out of 12, the size of a whole fleet.

## Winning
- The first to hit all 12 squares of the other fleet wins.
- If both fleets go down on the same volley, it is a draw.
- If your opponent leaves, you win.
- The host can then tap **NEW GAME**.

## Example
You fire at the fourth column, fourth row, while your opponent fires at your sea. Yours is a hit. Next volley you try the square to its right: another hit. Two more along that row and their Carrier is likely gone. Reach 12/12 and you win, unless their shot on that same volley finished your fleet too, which is a draw.

## Controls
- **Placing:** tap a ship's name, tap **ACROSS** or **DOWN** to turn it, then tap a square on your sea. The ship starts there and runs right or down.
- **RANDOMISE** lays out a new random fleet.
- **Firing:** tap a square on their sea. One tap fires and locks it in.
