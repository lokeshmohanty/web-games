# Five in a Row

Line up five of your marks on a board with no edges. Two to four players, each in their own colour and shape; bots can fill the empty seats, or the seats can stay empty.

## How to play
- Players take turns placing one mark each.
- The **first mark** of the game always goes in the **centre**.
- Every mark after that goes on an empty cell **touching a mark already down**: beside it, above or below it, or diagonally (any of its 8 neighbours). Any player's mark counts.
- The board grows wherever people play. There is no edge to run into.
- Who goes first changes every game, round the table.

## Winning
- Make an unbroken line of **five or more** of your own marks, across, down or diagonally, and you win at once. Six or more in a line counts too.
- Everyone else shares second place.
- If a player leaves, their marks stay on the board and the others play on. The last player left wins.
- A game that somehow reaches 500 marks with no line is a draw.

## Example
You have four marks in a row with both ends empty: an **open four**. Whoever moves next can block only one end, so on your next turn you complete five at the other. Stop an open three before it becomes an open four. With three or four players, the player who moves just before a threat is usually the one who has to block it.

## Controls
- **Tap** a cell to place your mark. On your turn every cell you may use has a small dot.
- **Drag** to look around. **Pinch**, the mouse wheel, or **+** and **−** zoom; **◎** shows every mark.
- Zoomed far out, the first tap shows your mark and a second tap on the same cell places it.
- Keyboard: arrow keys move a cursor, **Enter** places, **+** / **−** zoom, **0** shows every mark.
