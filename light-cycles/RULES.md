# Light Cycles

Ride around the arena leaving a wall behind you, and never hit a wall. The last rider still moving wins the round. Up to eight riders; bots can fill the empty seats.

## Setting up
- The setup shows only the first time: for a party's first round with friends. The host picks an **open arena** or **obstacles** — a few blocks of wall on the arena from the start, never in a rider's path off the line — and taps **START**. START waits until at least two riders are seated.
- **PLAY AGAIN** goes straight on with the same arena, and so does a round started alone or with bots (on the arena you last played; an open arena the first time).
- At the end the host sees **Next game: open arena · CHANGE** (tap SEE THE BOARD to reach it): CHANGE picks the arena for the next round.

## How to play
- Each round starts with a countdown: 3, 2, 1, GO. Where each rider starts is dealt afresh every round.
- Your rider never stops. It moves square by square across a 40 by 40 arena, leaving a solid wall behind it.
- You can turn left or right, but not straight back on yourself.
- You crash if you hit the edge, any wall (your own included, and the obstacles), or another rider head-on in the same square. A head-on crashes both.
- Riders start at ten squares a second and speed up a little as the round goes on.
- If you crash, you watch the rest of the round.

## Scoring
- Each round you win adds one to your count, shown beneath the arena. Wins carry over between rounds.
- Riding alone, your score is the time you survived, in seconds to the tenth (from GO; the countdown does not count). Signed in, it can go on the arcade's leaderboard.

## Winning
- The last rider moving wins the round.
- If the last riders crash at the same moment, nobody wins it.
- Riding alone, the round ends when you crash and shows how long you lasted.
- **PLAY AGAIN** starts the next round.

## Example
In a two-rider round you start on the left heading right, and your rival starts on the right heading left, ten rows lower (next round you may be the one on the right). You turn up early and run along the top, cutting the arena in two. Your rival is left in the smaller half, runs out of room and hits a wall. You are the last one moving, so your count goes to 1.

## Controls
- **Touch:** tap the left half of the arena to turn left, the right half to turn right. Turns are relative to the way you face.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys point you up, down, left or right. **A** turns left and **D** turns right.
