# Sudoku

Fill the grid so every row, column and 3 by 3 box holds 1 to 9 once each. Everyone races on the same puzzle, and the first to fill it in correctly wins. Bots can join the race, or you can play alone against the clock.

## How to play
- The host picks **Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard** or **Expert**. Harder puzzles give fewer digits, and Hard and Expert need more than spotting the only digit that fits.
- The host deals **TODAY'S PUZZLE**, the same for everyone that day at that level, or a **NEW PUZZLE**.
- Everyone solves on their own phone. The given digits cannot be changed.
- Wrong digits are not marked. If the grid is full but something is wrong, you are told so.
- Bars at the top show how many cells each racer has right.
- Bots fill in one correct cell every few seconds: faster on Easy, slower on Expert.

## Scoring
- In a race, what counts is how many cells you have right.
- Alone, what counts is your time. Your best time for each level is kept.

## Winning
- The first player to fill every empty cell correctly wins.
- Everyone else is placed by cells right. Equal counts share a place.
- Alone, you finish when the grid is right, and you see your time.

## Example
Row 1 reads 5, 3, blank, blank, 7, then four blanks. The first blank's column holds 1, 2 and 8, and its box holds a 9. With 3, 5 and 7 in the row, only 4 or 6 fits, so you pencil in both with **NOTES**. Later a 6 lands in that row, so you place the 4. Fill the last empty cell correctly before anyone else and you win.

## Controls
- Tap a cell, then a digit. Tap the same digit again, or **ERASE**, to clear it.
- **NOTES** toggles pencil marks. Placing a digit clears that note from its row, column and box.
- **Keyboard:** 1 to 9 to place, Backspace or 0 to erase, N for notes, arrows to move.
- Alone, **CONTINUE** picks up an unfinished puzzle.
