# Bubble Rush

Bubbles rise up one shared field. Tap them to pop them before anyone else does. The best score after sixty seconds wins. Up to eight players; bots can fill the empty seats.

## How to play
- Before each round the host picks **FREE-FOR-ALL** or **TEAMS** and taps **START**.
- A 3, 2, 1 countdown, then the round runs for 60 seconds.
- Bubbles rise from the bottom and wobble as they go. Tap one to pop it.
- The round speeds up as it goes. In the last 10 seconds (**FRENZY**) bubbles come even faster.
- If the host leaves the app, the round pauses for everyone until they come back.

## Scoring
- Small bubbles are worth **30**, medium ones **20**, big ones **10**.
- Golden bubbles, with sparkles round them, are worth **50**. They are quick.
- Spiky bombs cost you **25** and stun you for just over a second: your taps do nothing until it wears off.
- Every pop adds to your streak. Your streak sets a multiplier: **×2** after 4 pops in a row, **×3** after 8, **×4** after 12, and at most **×5** after 16.
- Your streak breaks, back to ×1, when you:
  - tap empty field (a miss),
  - pop a bomb,
  - go 1.5 seconds without popping anything.
- If someone else popped a bubble just before you, your tap does not count, but it does not break your streak either.

### Scoring example
You pop four small bubbles in a row: 30 + 30 + 30 + 30 = 120. Your streak is now 4, so the next pop is doubled: a medium bubble gives 20 × 2 = 40, and a golden one after it 50 × 2 = 100. You have 260. Then you tap a bomb: −25, so 235, your streak is back to nothing and you are stunned for a moment. Your next small bubble is worth 30 again.

## Teams
- In **TEAMS**, players on Team A and Team B (set in the party) play against each other. TEAMS can only be picked when both teams have someone on them.
- Plain bubbles come in the two teams' colours. Popping your own team's colour scores as usual.
- Popping the other team's colour scores nothing, but it keeps your streak going and takes that bubble away from them (**DENIED**).
- Golden bubbles and bombs belong to nobody: anyone can pop them.
- A team's score is the **average** of its players' scores, so a team of one can beat a team of three.
- A player who leaves during the round still counts in their team's average, but is not ranked.

## Winning
- When the clock runs out, the highest score wins. Equal scores share a place.
- In TEAMS, the team with the higher average wins.
- Playing alone, the end card shows your score.
- **PLAY AGAIN** goes back to the mode choice for the next round.

### Winning example
A teams round ends. Team A is Asha (300) and Ben (100), so its score is (300 + 100) ÷ 2 = 200. Team B is Chen alone with 240. Team B wins, 240 to 200, even though Asha had the best score of anyone.

## Controls
- **Touch or mouse:** tap a bubble to pop it.
- **Keyboard (host):** Space or Enter starts the round from the mode choice.
