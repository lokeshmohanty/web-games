# Liar's Dice

Bid on dice you cannot see, and call out the bids you think are lies. Be the last player with dice left. Two to six players; bots can fill the empty seats.

## How to play
- Everyone starts with five dice, rolled in secret. You see only your own.
- **Ones are wild:** a one counts as whatever face is being bid on.
- Players take turns. On your turn you either **bid** or call **LIAR!** on the last bid.
- A random player opens the first game. Each **NEW GAME** is opened by the next player round the table, so everyone gets to bid first.
- A bid names a count and a face from 2 to 6, such as "four 5s". It claims there are *at least* that many, ones included, across **everyone's** dice.
- Each bid must beat the last: more dice, or the same number of dice with a higher face. You cannot bid more dice than are on the table, or bid on ones.
- When someone calls LIAR!, every cup is lifted and the dice are counted.
- If there are at least as many as the bid said, the caller loses a die. Otherwise the bidder does.
- The player who lost a die starts the next round, or the caller if that player is out. Everyone rolls again.

## Scoring
- There are no points. What counts is how many dice you have left.
- Every cup is checked against what was rolled. A player caught changing their dice loses the die, whatever the count.

## Winning
- Lose your last die and you are out.
- The last player with dice wins. Everyone else shares second place.

## Example
Three players have 15 dice. You roll 1, 3, 5, 5, 6. Ana opens "three 5s". You hold three 5s yourself, counting your wild one, so you raise to "four 5s". Ben bids "four 6s": same count, higher face. Ana calls LIAR! There are only three 6s and ones in total, so Ben loses a die and starts the next round.

## Controls
- On your turn, tap a face from 2 to 6 and set the count with **−** and **+**. It starts at the smallest legal raise.
- Tap **BID** to bid, or **LIAR!** to call.
