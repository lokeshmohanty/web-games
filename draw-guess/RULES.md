# Draw & Guess

One player draws a secret word while everyone else races to guess it. Score the most points by guessing fast and drawing well. This game needs 2 to 8 people; there are no bots.

## How to play
- Players take turns to draw. Everyone draws twice, so a game has two rounds per player.
- At the start of each round the drawer is given a word. Only they can see it.
- The drawer has **60 seconds** to draw it. Everyone else sees a blank for each letter of the word.
- Guessers type guesses into the chat. A guess must be the exact word; capitals do not matter.
- Wrong guesses show in the chat for everyone. A right guess shows only as "guessed it".
- Once you have guessed right you stop guessing for that round. The drawer cannot guess.
- The round ends when the time runs out, or straight away once everyone has guessed. The word is then shown for five seconds.

## Scoring
- A right guess scores **100 points plus 2 for every second left** on the clock.
- The drawer scores **50 points** for each player who guesses the word.

## Winning
- After the last round, the player with the most points wins.
- Players on equal points share the place.

## Example
Three players means six rounds. Asha draws *rocket*, and the others see six blanks. Ben guesses "Rocket" with 45 seconds left and scores 100 + 90 = 190. Asha gets 50. Cara guesses it with 20 seconds left and scores 140, and Asha gets another 50. Everyone has guessed, so the round ends early. If Ben is still ahead after round six, he wins.

## Controls
- **Drawing:** drag a finger or the mouse on the board. Tap a colour dot to change pen, a size dot to change thickness, and **CLEAR** to wipe the board.
- **Guessing:** type in the box and tap **SEND**, or press Enter.
